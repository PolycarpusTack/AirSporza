01 · THE APPLICATION AND ITS PURPOSE

SporzaPlanner (Planza) is a broadcast planning system for a public broadcaster's sports desk. It manages events (fixtures to broadcast), channels (Eén, Canvas, VRT MAX), rights contracts, tech plans and crew assignments, with feed imports and real-time sync. It effectively runs two UIs today: the legacy amber-themed app (Planner, Sports, Contracts, Import) and the newer teal "Ops" shell (Schedule, Rundown, Rights, Registry, Sync) behind a feature flag.

The domain is genuinely a management sim: scarce resources (crew, OB vans, channel minutes), contracts with deadlines, a fixture calendar, and a weekly cycle of decisions. The FM framing is not a skin — it matches how planners actually work.
